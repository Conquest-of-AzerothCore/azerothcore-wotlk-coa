# 0320. Minion hits and reads in conformance checks

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-12

## Context

Necromancer tooltips state the hits and heals of raised minions: a Ghoul's Command hit and heal, the Ghoul's auto
attack heal, the Abomination's disease cloud. The minion casts those spells, not the player. The harness read a
spell's damage or healing events by the player as caster, by its pet, by another actor, or by a live owned creature
of an entry (`entry`, damage only). A control arm without the minion had no creature to resolve, so `entry` stopped
the scenario. The `script_hit` check read only the player's hits. Command: Ghouls 801514 has a damage effect and a
heal effect whose values the same tooltip states, and the hit model refused a spell with two hit effects. A
`metric_delta` read right after a gate learned at the start saw the state before the learned passive applied: Life
Force 525004 still read 2 stacks after Summoning Adept 92123 was learned. `prove` gave no way to pass
`--gameplay-db-workers` to the gameplay runs that share the test MySQL.

## Considered options

1. Read minion events with `owned_creature_damage_*` and `owned_creature_heal_*`. Rejected: they sum every spell and
   swing of the entry, so a Ghoul's auto attack heal and its Command heal cannot be told apart.
2. Resolve a live creature as `entry` does and let the control arm fail. Rejected: the control arm is the proof that
   the hit needs the minion.
3. Filter the recorded events by the caster's creature entry, and pass that filter through the hit checks. Chosen.

## Decision

- The harness metrics `spell_damage_count`, `spell_damage_total`, `spell_heal_count`, `spell_heal_total` and
  `spell_effective_heal_total` take `caster_entry`: they keep the events whose caster is a creature of that entry,
  resolved as the `owned_creature_*` metrics resolve it, and read 0 when none has cast. It replaces `entry`, `pet` and
  `caster`; `run.py validate` refuses the combination. It reads recorded events only.
- A `script_hit` entry takes `caster_entry` (its reads take the filter) and `hit_effect` (the hit model reads only the
  tooltip tokens of that effect of the hit spell, so one effect of a damage and heal spell is modelled).
- A `metric_delta` entry with `gate_timing: "start"` takes `gate_settle_ms`, a wait after the start gate is learned
  and before the first read.
- `conformance.py prove` takes `--gameplay-db-workers N` and passes it to every `coa-verify` run.
- Without the new fields every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19` and `--stack`).

## Consequences

- A minion's hit or heal is judged against the tooltip like a player's script-cast hit, with a control arm without
  the minion that must count no hit.
- The hits are read on the caster's combat log, so a scenario keeps one creature of the entry near the caster, as the
  `owned_creature_*` metrics require.
- The filter keeps any creature of the entry. A scenario that summons other creatures of the same entry would count
  them too.
