# 0321. Minion magnitudes on melee hits and cast-proc baselines

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-12

## Context

Animate: Tomb King 805044 states that every Undead minion's attack deals an additional 10% of its damage as Plague
damage (Putrid Claws 570050, cast by the minion). Side b's minion trigger action (ADR 0340) reads a minion's damage
with `owned_creature_damage_total`, which adds the minion's spell hits, Putrid Claws included, to its melee damage;
the magnitude read the observed spell as the caster's. The Tomb King lives 15 s and Putrid Aura 800027 20 s, far
shorter than one magnitude part. Tundra Warriors 92122 and Refreshing Chill 300940 proc on casts that deal no damage
(any spell, Command spells), and proc parts counted their trials as the cast's hits on the dummy. A cast-trigger proc
had no control arm without the passive.

## Considered options

1. Subtract the minion's spell hits from `owned_creature_damage_total`. Rejected: one read cannot separate them.
2. Set Putrid Aura on the minion with the harness. Rejected: the aura is the claim's subject (G-21, point 6).
3. Read the minion's melee alone, observe on the minion, cast the aura's spell again within its lifetime, and count
   cast-trigger trials as casts with a baseline before the passive. Chosen.

## Decision

- The harness metric `pet_melee_hits` counts a pet's or an entry's melee swings that hit (critical, glancing and
  plain), the swings `pet_melee_damage` sums.
- A `magnitude_ratio` trigger entry with a minion action may read `trigger_metric: "pet_melee_damage"` (counted by
  `pet_melee_hits`) and `observe_actor: "minion"` (the observed spell read with `caster_entry` of the minion entry).
- With `aura_cast`, `aura_recast_ms` casts the aura spell again (after `caster_power` and a cooldown reset) every that
  many ms during the minion's swings, and `presence_aura` names the aura whose presence the part reads.
- A proc entry with `trial_casts: true` counts its trials as casts of the trigger spell. `baseline: true` on a cast
  trigger casts it 20 times before the passive is learned; the judge requires no event there.
- A proc entry with a minion trigger may count `event_metric: "owned_creature_spell_proc_count"` with
  `event_actor: "minion"`: the native procs of the minion's copy of the aura (Ghoul Mastery 503740 on the Ghoul).
- Without the new fields every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19`).

## Consequences

- Putrid Claws is judged against the minion's own melee hits, and the Tomb King is kept up for the whole part.
- A cast-trigger proc has a control arm without the passive, as the minion procs have.
- `pet_melee_hits` is an event read (coverage `METRIC_EVIDENCE`).
