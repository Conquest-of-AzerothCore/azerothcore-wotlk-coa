# 0360. Minion stat reads and a raised minion before an uncast read

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Necromancer tooltips state stats of the raised minions: Unholy Frenzy 805029 raises their haste and movement speed,
the Undead stances change their movement speed, threat and armor, and Necromancy 804360 says they inherit attack
power, spell power, resistances, hit chance and haste from the player. These auras sit on the minions (effect 190,
SPELL_EFFECT_ASCENSION_APPLY_AURA_TO_SUMMONS), so a read of the player cannot see them. The harness read a live
owned creature's armor, health, swing interval and crit chance only. A `metric_delta` entry whose trigger is the
cast under test (`cast_in_before: false`) skipped the whole before phase, its setup casts included, so a read of a
minion stopped the control arm: no minion was there.

## Considered options

1. Read the aura amount on the minion. Rejected: a stored amount is a readback, not the unit's live value
   (G-21 point 4).
2. Raise the minion as the trigger and cast the spell under test as a setup cast. Rejected: the after phase raises a
   second minion after the cast, and a read by entry cannot tell the two apart.
3. Add read-only harness metrics on the live owned creature, and let the before phase keep its setup casts. Chosen.

## Decision

- The harness metrics `owned_creature_run_speed_rate`, `owned_creature_attack_power`, `owned_creature_spell_power`
  (`school_mask`, default Shadow), `owned_creature_resistance` (`school`), `owned_creature_melee_hit_chance`,
  `owned_creature_cast_speed`, `owned_creature_power` (`power`, default mana) and `owned_creature_threat` (the threat
  the creature named by `target` holds against it) read the live owned creature that `owned_creature_armor` resolves. They read and record only.
- `coverage.py` lists them, with `owned_creature_armor` and the swing interval and crit chance reads taken from side b
  of B1 (023bf68994), as state evidence with their promise words.
- A `metric_delta` entry with `cast_in_before: false` takes `setup_in_before: true`: the before phase still makes its
  setup casts, then reads. It needs setup casts and the default gate timing.
- Without the new field every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19` and `--stack`).

## Consequences

- A minion stat claim is judged on the live minion, with a control arm in which the minion exists without the cause.
- The after phase raises another minion of the same entry. The read takes the first one the grid search returns, so
  the cause must reach every minion of the entry (an aura cast after both are raised).
