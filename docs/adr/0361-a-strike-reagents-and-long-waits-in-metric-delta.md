# 0361. A strike, reagents and long waits in metric_delta

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Phylactery 500933 makes the Necromancer a Shade when a hit would take the caster below 10% health, protects the
Shade, stops its casts and attacks, revives it at the Phylactery and kills it otherwise. Each of these is a state
read after a hit the caster takes. `metric_delta` had no hit taken, `taken_hit` judges only damage and absorbs, and
the Phylactery needs two Corpse Dust 37201: no generated scenario carried a reagent, so the cast failed with
SPELL_FAILED_REAGENTS. The Phylactery lasts 120000 ms, and a `metric_delta` scenario kept the default 90000 ms
timeout, which caps every wait.

## Considered options

1. Read the state through `taken_hit`. Rejected: its judge reads strike damage, not a state after the strike.
2. Set the Shade with `set_aura`. Rejected: the Shade is the claim's subject (G-21 point 6).
3. A strike in `metric_delta`, the cast's reagents added, and a timeout that follows the waits. Chosen.

## Decision

- A `metric_delta` entry takes `strike` (`spell`, `health`, `max_health`, `count`, `wait_ms`, `dx`): the scenario
  adds the passive striker of `taken_hit` (Harvest Golem 36, `spell_hit_bonus` 100), and in each phase, after the
  setup casts and before the trigger cast, moves the caster by `dx`, sets its health and maximum, and has the striker
  cast `spell` at it `count` times, `wait_ms` apart.
- A `metric_delta` setup or trigger cast first adds the spell's reagents (`Reagent[0-7]`, `ReagentCount[0-7]`) to the
  caster.
- A `metric_delta` scenario whose waits pass 90000 ms takes a timeout of its waits plus the margin, up to the accelerated
  scenario cap, as `attack_swings` scenarios already did.
- A `cost_gate` entry takes `after_cast_gap_ms`, a wait after each of its `after_casts`, counted in the attempt's
  wait: Underking 704701 needs ten Command casts, each after the global cooldown.
- `coverage.py` lists `alive` and `can_attack` as state reads, promised by "die" and "attack". `health` stays event
  evidence, the health lost to an applied hit (ADR 0381).
- Without these fields every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19` and `--stack`).

## Consequences

- The caster's health is a precondition fixture (a stated health before a real hit); a verdict names it. The
  striker's spell is a stock spell, mitigated by the caster's armor.
- Reagents come from the client data; a reagent the harness cannot add stops the scenario, which shows in its
  result.
