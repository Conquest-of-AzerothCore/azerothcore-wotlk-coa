# 0362. A target health per hit_ratio arm

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Harvest Plague 583256: "The plague intensifies and siphons increased health based on how close to death your target
is." The tick of the 500968 family is multiplied by 2 - the target's health percent / 100
(AscensionNecromancerContracts.cpp:418-419). A `hit_ratio` entry held the dummy at one health percent for both arms,
so the two arms could not differ by target health. `set_health_pct` only lowers health, and the ticks lower the
dummy's health between trials.

## Considered options

1. A `metric_delta` of two absolute tick amounts. Rejected: each cast rolls 172 to 210, so the amounts are not
   deterministic.
2. A new tick-ratio scenario of two ticks of one application. Rejected for now: it needs a new scenario kind and judge.
3. A `target_health_pct` per `hit_ratio` arm, applied with `set_health` on the dummy before every trial's cast.
   Chosen.

## Decision

- A `hit_ratio` arm takes `target_health_pct` (between 0 and 100, exclusive): before every trial's observed cast the
  dummy's health is set to that percent of its 50000000 and the scenario waits 500 ms. It refuses swings, another
  target and a scenario-level `target_health_pct`.
- Without the field every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19` and `--stack`).

## Consequences

- The dummy health is a precondition fixture, named in the verdict. The claim's subject is the tick amount.
- A rolled tick needs a large spell power fixture to keep the trial count at the scenario's capacity (Harvest Plague:
  20000 spell power, 20 trials per arm).
