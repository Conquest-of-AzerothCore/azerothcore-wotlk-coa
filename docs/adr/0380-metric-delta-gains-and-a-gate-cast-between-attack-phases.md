# 0380. Metric-delta gains, ratios, setup targets and a gate cast between attack phases

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Two Necromancer claims need a before/after read that `metric_delta` could not judge.

- Bone Ward 681529: "melee and ranged damage taken reduces the target's attack power by
  ${-($704706m1+$704706ppl1)}" (223 at level 60). The attacker's attack power is the read. The dummy's own attack
  power read 38 in one run and 42 in the next (pair 20261112-235807 / -235926), and the metric floors at 0, so the
  stated reduction cannot be an exact absolute pair: it is a difference, read above the floor.
- Undead: Pacify 500983: "Your undead minions assume a passive behavior and will not attack enemies or cast
  spells." The minion only attacks when the caster fights, and the minion's hit count is cumulative. The `attack_swings`
  form (the caster attacks the dummy in each phase) learned its gate between the phases and refused setup casts, so it
  could neither raise the minion nor cast a stance.

## Considered options

1. Read the attack power aura's stored amount on the attacker. Rejected: a readback, not the live unit (G-21 point 4).
2. Fix the dummy's attack power with an absolute expectation. Rejected: its base reading differs between runs.
3. Judge the reduction on the difference, with a fixture that lifts the reading above the floor, and let the attack
   phases raise the minion once and cast the gate between them. Chosen.

## Decision

- `metric_delta` takes `expected_gain`: the verdict is `ok` when the after reading less the before reading equals it
  (within the entry's tolerance) and the before reading reaches `before_expected`, the least reading at which no floor
  of the metric can hide the gain. `expected` must equal `before_expected` plus the gain; it refuses `after_at_least`
  and an expiry read. Without the field the judge is unchanged.
- `metric_delta` takes `gate_cast` with `attack_swings`: the gate spell is learned with the setup spells before the
  first phase, the setup casts are made once at the caster before the first phase (`setup_wait_ms` after each), and
  the gate is cast at the caster between the phases instead of learned. `gate_cast` needs `attack_swings`; setup casts
  with `attack_swings` need `gate_cast`.
- `metric_delta` takes `expected_ratio` with a stated `tolerance`: `ok` when the after reading over the before
  reading is the ratio within the tolerance and the before reading reaches `before_expected`; `expected` must be
  `before_expected` times the ratio. It refuses `expected_gain`, `after_at_least` and an expiry read. Greater
  Skeletal Warrior's Pierced 570216 lowers the dummy's armor, 528 or 562 in different runs, by a factor of 0.9; the
  tolerance 0.0025 covers the truncation of an armor above 400.
- `metric_delta` takes `setup_target` (`caster`, `dummy` or `enemy`) for its setup casts, which otherwise go to the
  trigger's target: a raise is cast at the caster and the Command that follows at the dummy.
- `coverage.py` lists `cast_speed_multiplier` (a player's UNIT_MOD_CAST_SPEED) as state evidence promised by
  "casting speed" or "cast speed": Decaying Poison 805024 slows an enemy player's casting.
- Without the new fields every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19`, `--stack`, and
  every class 23 scenario of 044a45456d).

## Consequences

- Bone Ward's attack power reduction is read on a dummy that carries AP Test 56412 (+801) as a precondition fixture,
  around a real Bone Toss 707669 cast; the trigger from damage taken is its own trigger entry.
- Pacify's two claims read a Ghoul's hits and a Skeletal Mage's spell hits: at least one in the control phase, none
  added after the cast. A minion hit that lands in the step between the before read and the cast would alert a correct
  server; the gate cast follows the read by one step.
