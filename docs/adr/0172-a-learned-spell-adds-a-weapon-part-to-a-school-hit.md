# 0172. A learned spell adds a weapon part to a school hit

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Extends: ADR 0010 (weapon percent order), ADR 0078 (gated hits)

## Context

Chastise 503141, "$?s806525[ plus an additional $806525s1% Weapon Damage][]": its Effect[2]
SPELL_EFFECT_WEAPON_PERCENT_DAMAGE resolves to 0 until Martial Training 806525 is known, which adds 150 through
SPELLMOD_EFFECT3. The hit model took only the token-bearing school effect, so with 806525 known it had no weapon
part (`hit_mean` weapon None), and a hit ratio is not constant here: the weapon part does not scale with the school
part.

## Options

1. A hit ratio with fixed attack power and spell power, the expected ratio computed by hand. The two parts take
   different engine paths, so a hand ratio repeats the model outside the tool.
2. A hit model with both parts, computed as the engine does, for a rank and a learned spell named in the class file.

## Decision

Option 2. Class-file section `learned_hits` (`spell`, `rank`, `label_suffix`), generated by `--learned-hit`. The
scenario learns the spell; the model is the rank's school hit plus the reference weapon roll at the one percent the
tooltip shows for the learned spell (Spell::EffectWeaponDmg: weapon damage plus attack power / 14 x speed without
the total percent for a non-physical school, the percent truncated, then MeleeDamageBonusDone), each part with the
known modifiers and its own floor, the sum mitigated. The rank's own `hit_mean` is the control.

## Consequences

- Without a `learned_hits` entry every scenario regenerates byte-identical.
- Seeded tests: the summed mean of a constant school part and the weapon roll; a learned spell without a shown
  percent is unmodelled; with 1000 hits, a measured mean without the weapon part, and one with 100% instead of
  150%, alert.
