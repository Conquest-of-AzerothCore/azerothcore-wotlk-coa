# 0385. Stat attribution, threat per damage and cures by the debuffed unit

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

The B2 side b review asked for: Bone Ward's Stamina judged with G-22's attribution (aura absent before, present
after, the stat back after removal); Putrid Summoner's "Reduces your threat generated" judged on threat actually
added, not on the threat modifier; Plague of Undeath's dispel judged on a unit that is not the dispeller and on a
creature whose damage taken carries no player-against-player modifier (an enemy player took 225 of the 456).

## Decision

- `stat_gain` takes `attribute_stat: true` for the `stat` metric: the attribution reads (the cast's auras, the aura
  count, the cast count, the return after the removal) are made with `Unit::GetStat` as the named read. Without the
  field nothing changes.
- `coverage.py` lists `threat_per_damage` (the creature's threat on a unit over the health it lost) as state
  evidence promised by "threat"; a Lichfrost hit on a fresh dummy after Undead: Protect reads 0.25 with Putrid
  Summoner and 1.0 without.
- `metric_delta` takes `target_casts` (the dummy casts the spells at itself after each trigger cast) and
  `helper_casts` (a second enemy player of the enemy's faction casts them at the enemy).
- Without the new fields every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19`, `--stack`).
