# 0034. Solar Champion's threat reduction reaches the damage spells of Radiant Flame and Sun Down

- Status: accepted (mentor D-185, confirmed defect under the tooltip, 2026-11-22)
- Date: 2026-11-22

## Context

Solar Champion 504734: "Increases the range of your Piety spells by $s1% and reduces the threat generated by them by
$s2%." Effect[1] is aura 108 (ADD_PCT_MODIFIER), MiscValue 2 (SPELLMOD_THREAT), BasePoints -21 + 1 = -20%, with the
class mask [0x08000000, 0x01080542, 0]. The mask names Radiant Flame 560867 (SpellFamilyFlags[0] 0x08000000) and Sun
Down 572752 (SpellFamilyFlags[1] 0x01000000), but neither deals its own damage:

- 560867 Effect[0] aura 227 triggers 807058 each second; 807058 carries SpellFamilyFlags [0, 0, 0x8];
- 572752 ends in 572753 (cast by the class on expiry, `AscensionSunClericAuras.cpp:173-176`); 572753 carries
  SpellFamilyFlags [0, 0, 0x40].

`ThreatManager::CalculateModifiedThreat` applies SPELLMOD_THREAT with the spell that deals the damage
(`ThreatManager.cpp:713`, stock), so the cut missed both. Pairs x2, x3 and x4 read Radiant Flame's threat per damage
1.0053..1.0058 with the talent, equal to the control, against 0.80625. Horusath Blast, Rapture and Sunflare, which deal
their own damage, read 0.80.

## Options

1. Add the triggered spells' bits to word 2 of 504734's Effect[1] mask in the class contracts.
2. Give 807058 and 572753 their parents' family flags. Rejected: every other modifier keyed to those flags (damage,
   crit, cost) would then reach the triggered spells.
3. Change `ThreatManager.cpp:713` to read the triggering spell. Rejected: a shared engine path.

## Decision

Option 1. `AscensionSunCleric::ApplyContracts` adds 0x8 | 0x40 to word 2 of 504734's Effect[1] class mask
(`AscensionSunClericContracts.cpp:285-286`). The range effect (Effect[0]) keeps its DBC mask.

## Consequences

- With the talent, Radiant Flame's ticks and Sun Down's strike generate 20% less threat; the flat initial threat of
  each cast (`Spell::HandleThreatSpells`, `Spell.cpp:5736-5737`, added with ignoreModifiers at `Spell.cpp:5766`) is not
  cut, as for the other Piety spells.
- Spells reached by the new bits: a DBC scan of SpellFamilyName 33 for SpellFamilyFlags[2] & 0x48 finds 807058,
  572753 and Vow of Light 505197. 505197 is a Sun Cleric copy spell, which the contracts mark
  SPELL_ATTR3_IGNORE_CASTER_MODIFIERS (`AscensionSunClericContracts.cpp:64-68`), and `SpellInfo::IsAffectedBySpellMod`
  refuses every non-duration modifier for such a spell (`SpellInfo.cpp:1566-1570`), so the cut does not reach it.
  807058 is triggered only by the Radiant Flame ranks 560862-560867 and 806060, 572753 only by Sun Down. No other
  class's spell and no shared engine path changes.
- Sun Down's threat_slope entries read with a flat of 178, the SpellLevels of 572752 (59), 572753 (60) and 572754
  (59), each cast adding its own once (the earlier 119 left out 572754 and voided pair y1).
- No engine file, no SQL and no client text change.

## Verification

See the class conformance report of this fix: the counted pair at level 60 on the fix build, Radiant Flame
(`threat_radiant`) and Sun Down (`threat_slope_sundown`) expected 0.8 from the tooltip, Horusath Blast, Rapture and
Sunflare unchanged at 0.8, and the controls without the talent at 1.0 in band.
