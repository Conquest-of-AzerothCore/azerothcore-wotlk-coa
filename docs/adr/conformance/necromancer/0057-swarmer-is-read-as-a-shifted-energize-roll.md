# 0057. Swarmer is read as a shifted energize roll

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Claim 160.0, Swarmer 704528 "Increases the Runic Power generated by Crypt Swarm by $/10;s1 per tick.": Effect[0] aura
107 SPELL_AURA_ADD_FLAT_MODIFIER, MiscValue 12 (SPELLMOD_EFFECT2), +20 (BasePoints 19 + 1, shown as 2), class mask
word 2 524288, which is Crypt Swarm's energize 800344 SpellFamilyFlags[2]. 800344 Effect[1] SPELL_EFFECT_ENERGIZE
rolls 30..80 internal Runic Power per tick (BasePoints 29 + DieSides 51), so no exact total proves +20. The
`energize_range` kind (ADR 0549) already proves a roll by its ends on single events, but learned only its cast spell.

## Options

- A mean-difference judge on two arms: a new kind, and the mean of a 51-value roll needs many events for a 20-unit
  band.
- The existing `energize_range` with an optional `learn` list: the talent shifts both ends of the roll by 20, so the
  treatment reads 50..100 and a control without the talent reads 30..80.

## Decision

`energize_range` takes `learn` (a list, learned right after `cast_spell`). Two entries on Crypt Swarm 501886, 80 casts,
13 polls of 250 ms per cast, The End Is Near 680486 for the mana cost: `energize_range` (704528 learned, 50..100) and
`energize_control` (no talent, 30..80). Each needs 384 single events with both ends seen.

## Consequences

- A missing +20 alerts the treatment (amounts below 50), a +20 without the talent alerts the control (above 80), and a
  wrong amount moves an end out of the roll.
- Pilot 20261113-202202: 480 single events per arm, 50..100 and 30..80, both ends seen.
- Templar has no `energize_range` entry; Templar `generate --class 19` and `--stack` are byte-identical to 628582dc78.
