# 0004. Potent Mixes lowers threat through the threat percent aura

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-16

## Context

Written 2026-11-21, after the fix, as a catch-up for guide section 7.

Potent Mixes 503748 and 504888 "reduces the threat generated by your spells by 15% / 30%". Their threat effect
(effect 1) is aura 103 SPELL_AURA_MOD_TOTAL_THREAT, a flat modifier written only to threat references that exist when
the aura changes (`ThreatManager.cpp:809-829`), so a passive learned before combat never lowered any threat. Cycle 5
measured 1.14 threat per damage with 504888 against 1.13 without. The mentor classed it a confirmed defect (M-36).

## Decision

- `ApplyContracts` turns effect 1 of both spells from aura 103 into aura 10 SPELL_AURA_MOD_THREAT for all schools
  (`SPELL_SCHOOL_MASK_ALL`), the form stock threat talents use.
- All schools: the tooltip says "your spells" and names none (contributor decision 2026-11-22).

## Consequences

- Regression: catalog `witch-doctor-potent-mixes-threat-multiplier` (the threat multiplier reads 1 without the talent,
  0.85 with rank 1, 0.7 with rank 2). It passed in verify_all 20261117-074923.
