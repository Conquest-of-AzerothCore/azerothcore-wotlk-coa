# 0143. A short expiry pair is timed from an instant cast

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-11

## Context

Beckon 804914, "Taunt an enemy to attack you for $d", states a 3000 ms end (DurationIndex 27). Its clause had only
the `aura_duration_ms` readback, and the gap batch's first attempt declared no behaviour route: `expiry.margins_of`
refuses an end shorter than about 4.5 s, because its placement reserves 4 steps before and 7 after the stated end
at 25 ms each, against a shift of `max(5% of E, 50 ms)` = 150 ms.

The judge (`expiry.arm_reading`, ADR 0126) itself needs less. Timed from the act with no assert after it, the slack
is the act's own `elapsed_ms`, and the before reading's window is two steps wide and the after reading's four (each
step adds `max_step_ms`). The recorded runs show an instant cast, a wait and an `aura` reading all completing within
their own tick (`elapsed_ms` 0, waits exact, `max_step_ms` 25: `.cache/verify-all/20261111-124539`, expiry of
705263). An instant spell with no travel time applies its aura inside that cast step: `Spell::prepare` calls
`cast(true)` when the cast time is 0 (`Spell.cpp:3778-3779`) and `Spell::cast` delays only a spell with `Speed > 0`
(`Spell.cpp:4106`).

A real-clock reference run is not a substitute: it measures at world-tick resolution, normal verification is
accelerated only (`docs/coa/verification.md`, "Real-clock reference runs"), and there is no discrepancy to
investigate.

## Decision

- `expiry.act_margins_of(E)` places the before reading `floor(shift) - 26` ms before `E` and the after reading 26 ms
  after it, and raises `NotModelled` when the before margin is not above two steps or the after margin not below
  `shift` - four steps (an end under about 2.54 s).
- `expiry.act_anchored_arm` builds such an arm with no applied assert; `cast_arm` then puts the pair right after the
  act.
- A single-arm `cast_applies_aura` entry uses it only when the standard placement raises `NotModelled` and the cast
  spell applies its own aura within its cast step: the applied aura is the cast spell, its cast time is 0 and its
  Speed 0 (`expiry.lands_within_its_cast_step`). Otherwise the entry stays unmodelled.
- The `cast_applies_aura` readback reads `aura_duration_ms` on the entry's `target`, not always on the caster.
- The judge and its `shift` are unchanged; a run whose act or readings take longer reads `unmeasured`.

## Consequences

- Beckon's expiry pair reads the taunt aura on the dummy 2876 ms and 3026 ms after the cast; an `ok` excludes an end
  at or before about 2876 ms or after about 3126 ms, so a server 150 ms off alerts.
- Every existing scenario is generated byte for byte as before. Class 19 regenerated from `git archive` copies of
  b67a092ed1 and e066d2c8d3: the 1288 scenarios of the first have the same sha256 in the second, which adds only
  Beckon's readback and expiry.
- A spell with a cast time, travel time or a triggered aura still needs the standard placement.
