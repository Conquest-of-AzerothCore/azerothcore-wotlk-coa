# 0381. Health-loss reads, enemy casts and other metric-delta targets

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

- Putrid Summoner 560595 with Undead: Pacify: "Reduces your damage taken by $s2%." The reduction is taken in
  `necromancer_defense::OnDamage` (AscensionNecromancerEvents.cpp:330-337), which `Unit::DealDamage` calls
  (Unit.cpp:1135) after the spell damage log is built. A `taken_hit` damage ratio read from the log measured 1100
  against 1100 (pair 20261113-000828 / -000904) while the caster lost 990.
- Ghoulify 800706 is "Only usable on Humanoids"; its 40 s fear needs a Humanoid creature, and the shared dummy is a
  Mechanical. Its transform is a display change of the target.
- Plague of Undeath 503640: "If Plague of Undeath is dispelled, the enemy takes ... and is stunned for ...": the
  debuffed unit must remove the disease itself.
- Skeletal Rogue 500969: "Always strikes 5 times on enemies below 35% health": the Command's target must be below a
  health percent.

## Considered options

1. Read the damage-taken calculation (`script_spell_damage_taken`). Rejected: a readback (ADR 0153).
2. A new kind per claim. Rejected: each case is one more read or step in an existing kind.
3. Small optional fields on `taken_hit` and `metric_delta`, and two evidence rows. Chosen.

## Decision

- `taken_hit` with `judge: "damage_ratio"` takes `health_loss: true`: each trial also reads the caster's health after
  the strike, and the hit ratio uses the health lost from the health the trial sets (`caster_health_pct`) as each
  landed strike's damage. It needs one strike per trial and no cast inside a trial. `coverage.py` lists `health` as
  event evidence: the health lost to an applied hit.
- `metric_delta` takes:
  - `target_entry`: the creature entry of the dummy fixture (Kobold Vermin 6, a Humanoid);
  - `enemy_casts`: spells the enemy player learns before the base steps end and casts at itself after each trigger
    cast and its wait, `enemy_cast_wait_ms` after each (Cure Disease 528); with `enemy_casts_in_before` the enemy also
    casts them in an uncast before phase, so a heal the enemy casts is read without and then with the debuff
    (Renew 25222 under Plague of Undeath, judged as a ratio of the cumulative heal);
  - `target_health_pct`: `set_health_pct` on the trigger's target (the dummy or the enemy) right before each trigger
    cast.
- `coverage.py` lists `display_id` as state evidence, promised by "transform".
- Without the new fields every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19`, `--stack`, and
  every class 23 scenario of 044a45456d).

## Consequences

- A damage-taken modifier applied after the log (a script hook on `DealDamage`) is judged on the health the unit
  actually lost.
- The enemy player's own casts are real client casts of a learned spell, so its cost and target checks apply.
