# 0363. Dodged minion swings and a player rating override

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Necromancy 804360: "They also inherit ... and gain expertise from your hit chance." The minion's dodge and parry
chance is cut by the caster's melee hit chance x 100 (AscensionNecromancerContracts.cpp:486-500). Every generated
player carries the class file's 10000 melee hit rating, so no scenario had a caster without hit chance, and the harness
read the victim state of a melee swing only when the reading player was the victim, so a minion's dodged swing against
the dummy was not counted.

## Considered options

1. Read the outcome roll through a harness probe. Rejected: the cut is a script hook on the roll, with no use-time
   value to read.
2. A rate judge over two arms of one scenario that differ in the caster's hit chance. Rejected: the player fixture is
   set once per scenario.
3. Count dodged and parried minion swings, and let a `metric_delta` entry override the player's ratings for its
   scenario: one entry without hit rating (swings avoided), one with the class file's (none avoided). Chosen.

## Decision

- The harness parses the victim state of every `SMSG_ATTACKERSTATEUPDATE` a player receives, and counts per attacker
  the swings the victim dodged or parried. `owned_creature_avoided_count` (`entry`) sums them over the creatures of
  that entry; it reads recorded packets only. `coverage.py` lists it as event evidence.
- A `metric_delta` entry takes `player_ratings`: ratings of the class file's `ratings` set to other values for its
  scenario.
- Without the new fields every existing scenario is generated byte-identical (`generate --class 19` and `--stack`).

## Consequences

- The rating override is a precondition fixture, named in the verdict; each entry has its own control phase, and the
  pair of entries carries the contrast in the caster's hit chance.
- The parse of the victim state moved out of the victim branch; the victim reads are unchanged.
