title: Heirloom armor tooltips show the real armor type and turn red when you can't wear it
---
Plate heirlooms such as Strengthened Stockade Pauldrons used to read "Leather" on leather classes, so they looked wearable but could not be equipped. The tooltip now shows Plate, in red if your class can't wear it.
