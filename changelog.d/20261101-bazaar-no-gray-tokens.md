title: No Bazaar Tokens from gray creatures or from pet-only kills
---
Killing a creature that is gray for your level, including a dungeon or raid boss, no longer earns Bazaar Tokens. A player outside a group also earns none for a creature only their pets, guardians or summons hurt, so leaving them to fight alone no longer farms tokens. Green creatures and above, and kills where you hit the creature yourself, pay as before.
