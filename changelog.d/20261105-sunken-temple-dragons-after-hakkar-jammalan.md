title: Sunken Temple's dragons appear only after the Avatar of Hakkar and Jammal'an
---
Morphaz, Hazzas, Weaver and Dreamscythe stay hidden and cannot be pulled until the Avatar of Hakkar and Jammal'an the
Prophet are dead, as on Ascension. Once both have fallen, the four dragons appear and can be fought as before.
