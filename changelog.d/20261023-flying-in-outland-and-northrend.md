title: Flying works again in Outland and Northrend, and no mount flies in Azeroth
---
Flying mounts fly again with Expert Riding or better in Outland, and in Northrend once you know Cold Weather Flying. Every mount, including Ascension's own mounts such as Ash'adar, stays on the ground everywhere else, and a rider who leaves a flying area is dismounted. Server owners can restore the flight rules from before
this change with `CoA.LegacyMountFlight = 1` in `coa.conf`.
