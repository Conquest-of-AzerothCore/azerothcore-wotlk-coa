title: Bazaar Tokens from creatures have an hourly cap
---
Ordinary creatures pay Bazaar Tokens in full for the first 60 tokens an hour. Above that each token drops only a quarter of the time, and after 80 tokens in the hour creatures pay none until the hour is over. Logging out does not reset the hour. Bosses and quests are not limited and do not count towards the cap.
