title: Scaled loot previews survive a map change
---
The corpse loot tooltip and the Need/Greed roll frame preview scaled gear at the character's level again after
entering a dungeon, leaving one or any other map change. The server now resends the loot preview setting and the
levels of carried scaled items each time the character enters a map, not only at login.
