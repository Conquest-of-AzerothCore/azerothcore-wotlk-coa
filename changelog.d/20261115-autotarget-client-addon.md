title: AutoTarget add-on targets an enemy for you when you press an attack
---
The optional AutoTarget client add-on (off by default) targets an enemy when you press a damaging ability with no living enemy target: your target, else the enemy under the mouse, else the enemy nearest the middle of the screen, else the nearest enemy. Heals, buffs and macros are untouched. Copy `client-addons/AutoTarget` into `Interface/AddOns` and turn it on in Esc > Interface > AddOns.
