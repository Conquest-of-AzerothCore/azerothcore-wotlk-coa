title: Vanilla dungeon bosses no longer sleep or mind control tanks and healers
---
In vanilla 5-player dungeons, on every difficulty, bosses aim their sleeps, mind controls and similar abilities only
at players whose specialization neither tanks nor heals, as on Ascension. Without such a player the ability is not
cast, so a tank and healer pair is never slept or mind controlled. This covers abilities such as Jammal'an's Hex, the
Avatar of Hakkar's Cause Insanity, Shade of Eranikus' Deep Slumber, Zevrim Thornhoof's Sacrifice, Darkmaster
Gandling's Shadow Portal and Lady Illucia Barov's Dominate Mind and Fear. Jammal'an also no longer hexes a player
fighting him alone. Archmage Arugal's curses and the splash of Krastinov's Ghoulish Potion still reach anyone.
