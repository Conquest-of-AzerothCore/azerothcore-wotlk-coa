title: Weapon procs fire at the rate their tooltip states
---
Chance-on-hit weapons whose tooltip states a rate in procs per minute, such as Sludge Hammer (1ppm), Ghost Buster (5ppm),
Cursed Ritual Carver (3ppm) and Carapace Cracker (2.2 to 2.6ppm), ignored that rate. Those that previously proc'd on
every swing now proc at the stated rate, and those that fell back to a rate set by weapon speed now follow the tooltip.
