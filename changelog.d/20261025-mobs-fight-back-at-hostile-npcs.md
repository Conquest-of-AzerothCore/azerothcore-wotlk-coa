title: Mobs fight back when hostile NPCs attack them and stop chasing players who get away
---
A Scourge ghoul hit by Scarlet Crusade NPCs used to ignore them and keep chasing you far past its normal range. Now the NPCs can take its attention, and once an NPC is fighting it you leave combat as soon as you are 50 yards away.
