title: Archmage Arugal no longer mind-controls a player who fights him alone
---
In Shadowfang Keep, Archmage Arugal casts Arugal's Curse and Curse of Arugal only while at least two players fight
him. A player who solos him, alone or in a group whose other members are elsewhere, is never mind-controlled. In a
group fight his curses still take any player, tanks and healers included.
