title: Pet whistles that cannot summon their beast are no longer offered
---
Beastmaster's Whistles whose beast has no pet family or cannot be tamed were always refused as a Tamed Beast
appearance. The Vanity tab no longer lists or delivers them and Tiraxis no longer stocks the seven Mana Ray whistles.
Copies you already own stay in your bags.
