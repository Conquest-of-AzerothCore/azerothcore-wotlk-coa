title: Pyromancer's Phoenix is no longer oversized
---
Phoenix Egg's Phoenix now uses Ascension's own Phoenix model at about half the player's height, instead of
Kael'thas's raid Phoenix, which towered over its owner and hid other pets. It also follows on its owner's right,
so it no longer sits on top of a companion pet, which follows behind-left. No change to its abilities, healing or
commands.
