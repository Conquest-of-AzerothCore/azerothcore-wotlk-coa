title: Classic dungeons scale for everyone, even with a level 60 leading the group
---
Classic dungeons on Normal now scale to each player's level no matter what anyone's open world scaling setting is. Before, a group led by a level 60 with open world scaling turned off saw every dungeon creature at its normal level, so lower-level members faced creatures far above them.

Weapons and armor that drop in these dungeons now scale down to the level of the player who loots them, stats included, but never above the item's own level: a level 30 looting a level 40 item gets a level 30 version, and a level 60 gets the item as it is. Loot outside these dungeons is unchanged.
