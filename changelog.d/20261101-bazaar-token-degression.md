title: Open-world Bazaar Tokens pay less the more you farm the same creature
---
Killing the same creature over and over in the open world now pays fewer Bazaar Tokens each time: a
"heat" builds per creature and recovers over time on its own, tied to that creature's respawn delay, so
moving on to something else keeps paying normally. Dungeon and raid bosses, and anything in an instance or
battleground, are unaffected. On by default; turn it off with `EtherealBazaar.Tokens.Degression.Enable = 0`.
