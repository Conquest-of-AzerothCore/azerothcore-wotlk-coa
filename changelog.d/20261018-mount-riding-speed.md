title: Every mount's speed follows your riding skill, and buffs only show flying where it applies
---
Mounts now move at 40% with Novice Riding, 60% with Apprentice and 100% with Journeyman or higher. Flying mounts fly only with Expert (150%), Artisan (280%) or Extraordinary Riding (310%) where flying is allowed, and their buff shows a flying speed only once you know Expert Riding.
