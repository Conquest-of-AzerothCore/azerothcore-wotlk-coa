title: Low-level Bloodforged items no longer have inflated stats
---
Bloodforged items such as the Bloodforged Arced War Axe (+25 Strength, +21 Stamina at level 20) could carry two to three times the stats they should have. 72 of them, mostly between level 15 and 50, are corrected to Ascension's own values (for that axe, +9 Strength and +8 Stamina). If an item's tooltip still shows the old stats, clear your WDB cache.
