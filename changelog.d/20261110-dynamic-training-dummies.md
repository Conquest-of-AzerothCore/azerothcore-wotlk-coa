title: Practice with level-scaling training dummies in faction capitals
---
Capital training dummies scale to the player who engages them. Stormwind and Orgrimmar also offer tanking, healing, execute and grouped target practice. Each city has a Boss Simulator with a heavy strike to stun or disarm and a Shadow Bolt to interrupt after its immunity phase. Use /poke to end tank or boss practice, or to toggle the healing dummy.
