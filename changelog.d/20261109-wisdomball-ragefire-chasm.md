title: Wisdomball lists Ragefire Chasm quests once and leaves the Lost Satchel to Maur Grimtotem
---
The Wondrous Wisdomball no longer offers quests the game marks as unavailable, such as the older copy of The Power
to Destroy... in Ragefire Chasm, so each Ragefire Chasm quest is listed once.
A carried quest that has no objective of its own and ends at a character inside a dungeon, such as Searching for
the Lost Satchel, is no longer listed or handed in at the ball. It is handed in where the quest ends.
