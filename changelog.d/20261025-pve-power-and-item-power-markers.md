title: PvE Power now changes combat, and PvE and PvP Power show exact percentages
---
At level 60, each point of PvE Power adds 0.05% to your damage against creatures and takes 0.05% off the damage they deal to you, up to 495 points. Healing and shields get 0.02% per point in dungeons and raids. The tooltips on the character sheet now show the exact figure (24.75% at the cap) instead of rounding it down. Bloodforged gear no longer gives PvE Power, and a few hundred items that were missing their PvE or PvP Power now have it.
