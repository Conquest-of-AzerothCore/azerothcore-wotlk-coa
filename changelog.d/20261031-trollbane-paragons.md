title: The Trollbane trial's troll Paragons are back
---
Hardcore - Trollbane asked players to kill three troll Paragons that didn't exist, so the trial could not be
completed. Vazul, the Witherbark Paragon, now waits in the Witherbark Village cave in Arathi Highlands with two
Elite Witherbark Headhunters. Zazera, the Sandfury Paragon, stands on top of Sandsorrow Watch in Tanaris. Yahola,
the Mossflayer Paragon, holds the Mazra'Alor temple steps in the Eastern Plaguelands with her Left and Right Hand.
Vazul casts Chains of Ice and Shadow Bolt Volley. Zazera burns with Burning Sands and opens with Firestorm.
Splash her from one of the two Water Buckets on her tower to put her out and stun her; she rekindles 30 seconds
later, and each bucket takes 40 seconds to return, so alternate between them. Yahola casts Spectral Bolt and
Spectral Volley and raises Risen Servants with Army of the Dead early in the fight.
