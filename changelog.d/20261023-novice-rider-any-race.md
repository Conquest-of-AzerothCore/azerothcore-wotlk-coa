title: Every race can learn Novice Riding and get the Novice Rider quest near its starting zone
---
Riding trainers now teach every race of their faction, so a Night Elf who starts in Dun Morogh can train with Ultham Ironhorn or Binjy Featherwhistle. Garion Hunter in Goldshire, Hank Dullahan in Sentinel Hill and Gorn Axefist in Durotar now offer and accept Path to Ascension: Novice Rider, and the quest can be started from the Path to Ascension book again.
