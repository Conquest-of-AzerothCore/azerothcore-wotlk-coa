title: Bazaar Tokens need a fair kill, and pay less
---
Kills far below your level no longer pay Bazaar Tokens: while levelling, grey creatures pay nothing, and at level 60 a creature must be within five levels of you. Kills made only by your pets, guardians or summons still pay tokens, also outside a group. Token rates are lower (creature 0-2, dungeon boss 6-12, raid boss 30-40, quest 0-6) and the level-60 bonus is gone.
