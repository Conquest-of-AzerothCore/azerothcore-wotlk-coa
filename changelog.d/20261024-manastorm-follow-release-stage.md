title: Manastorm cache rewards can follow the realm's content release stage
---
Set Manastorm.FollowReleaseStage = 1 to gate Heroic Dungeon, Mythic Dungeon and mapped raid caches by the shared content release stage. Closed tiers fall back to the highest eligible lower DB bracket, preserving depth unlocks. Greedy Demon loot uses the released enabled caches' item-level ceiling. Off by default; disabled means no release-stage item-level cap. Segment selection and cache contents are unchanged.
