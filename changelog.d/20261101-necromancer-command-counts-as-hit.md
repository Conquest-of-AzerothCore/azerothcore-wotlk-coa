title: Necromancer Command orders count as your own hit
---
A Necromancer who orders their minions with a Command (Ghouls, Gargoyle, Bone Smash, Blight, Undead and others) now gets experience and loot from the creature the minions kill, even if they never attacked it themselves. Before, Commands that target the Necromancer rather than the enemy were not counted as a hit, so only the first kill with a swing or a damaging spell paid. Minions left to fight on their own without a Command still earn nothing.
