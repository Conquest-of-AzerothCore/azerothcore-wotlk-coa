title: Cultist Void-Touched marks every direct heal, including Blade of Yogg-Saron
---
Void-Touched now applies its stack on every direct heal from the Cultist, including heals on an ally or on the
Cultist himself at full health and the heal from Blade of Yogg-Saron, so the next Gaze of C'Thun consumes those
stacks and heals for them.
