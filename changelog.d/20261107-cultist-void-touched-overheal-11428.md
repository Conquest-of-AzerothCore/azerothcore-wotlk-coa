title: Cultist Void-Touched marks every direct heal, including Blade of Yogg-Saron and Shadow Pennant
---
Void-Touched now applies its stack on every direct heal from the Cultist, including heals on an ally or on the
Cultist himself at full health and the heals from Blade of Yogg-Saron and Shadow Pennant, so the next Gaze of
C'Thun consumes those stacks and heals for them.
