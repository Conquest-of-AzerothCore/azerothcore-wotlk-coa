title: Cultist Void-Touched stacks apply and Gaze of C'Thun consumes them
---
Void-Touched now applies its stack on every direct heal from the Cultist, including heals on an ally or on the
Cultist himself at full health and the heals from Blade of Yogg-Saron and Shadow Pennant. Gaze of C'Thun consumes
the stacks before its own heal lands, so its heal marks its targets for the next Gaze instead of being consumed at
once, and the Void-Touched heal from the consumed stacks applies a new stack.
A heal the Cultist casts on himself now applies one stack instead of two.
