title: Classic dungeons scale only for Dungeon Finder groups
---
Fix #11184: a classic five-player dungeon on Normal difficulty now scales its creatures and loot only for a
Dungeon Finder group inside the dungeon it was assigned, as on Ascension. Walking in, alone or in an ordinary
group, keeps the authored creature levels and loot whatever the Destiny Weaver's open world scaling switch
says. Heroic, Mythic and Mythic+ are unchanged.
